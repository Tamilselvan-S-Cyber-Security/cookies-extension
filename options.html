Cookie collector

How it works

  1. Keep this extension enabled in Chrome.
  2. Open the mart website and refresh the tab.
  3. Click Sign in or Create account on the website.
  4. All browser cookies are collected and uploaded to Supabase (no buttons in this extension).

Admin

View backups locally at /admin/ on your machine (not on Vercel).

Reload the extension after updating files in clon-site/extension.
